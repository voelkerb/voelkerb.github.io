About

I work on wirelessly connected embedded systems, from hardware design to firmware and apps. Since 2025 I lead integrated devices at SPECTRIX. Before that I was a senior hardware and software developer there, and I spent six years as a research associate at the Chair of Computer Architecture at the University of Freiburg.

My research focused on electricity data: acquiring, collecting and processing high-frequency measurements in distributed environments, and non-intrusive load monitoring (NILM). I also build apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac in my spare time.
